## Direction contract

THESIS: The plan as a National Park Service Unigrid brochure: a black title band on every page and the household's savings drawn as a trail map with numbered stops. It refuses the advisor-software report (navy and gold, pie charts, a success gauge, stock photos).

OWN-WORLD: White pages under a black band (title left; RetCalc logo and wordmark plus the page number right). Map land #EEF1E6 behind the signature charts, forest/woods greens for history ranges, trail red #B23A22 for the plan line, phase colors forest, sand, clay, water blue, dusk purple. Public Sans throughout, 800 for heads, tabular lining figures. Numbered black stop markers. Estimated figures carry a dashed underline; defaults and estimates carry word tags.

STORY: Pages 2 to 8 tell the household where it stands, the road by age, whether it lasts against history, where the money comes from, conditional pages (health before Medicare and the like), the score and the next moves. Pages 9 and 10 are the advisor's appendix: answers, method, glossary, year by year.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cover: tall black band with the household name at 46px, the date line under it, logo and tagline right; a full-width map of savings from today's age to the plan's end with the historical range and six numbered stops, a white legend box with a scale bar; below, three columns: points along the way, will it last plus readiness, your number plus Social Security; a black footer band with the disclaimer.
