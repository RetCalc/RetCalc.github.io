## Direction contract

THESIS: The tools, laid along a life. A vertical age line runs down the page and each tool hangs off it at the age you would use it, so the page shows how one household moves through the whole suite. It refuses the category default of a split hero over a row of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite (dark) and paper (light) grounds; amber for the key figure, the primary button and the line's start; tiles on Surface with hairline rules, the three main paths in tinted fields of amber, jade and steel. IBM Plex Sans at display scale (about 112px) with tabular figures. Every visual is a live SVG view of a real tool, drawn from the engine's 1926–2025 data or a labeled example household ($60,000 saved, $1,500 a month, Balanced: $1.57M at 65 from the Basic engine).

STORY: A first-time visitor learns RetCalc is free, private retirement planning checked against every market since 1926; sees the Calculator, the Guide, early-retirement, Social Security, Drawdown and RMD tools in life order; then the everyday money tools; and leaves through the Calculator, the Guide or the Drawdown Simulator.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One pinned header row (logo, Guide / Calculator / Tools / About, theme toggle). Left: "Know your" over an amber "number." at about 112px. Right, bottom-aligned: a one-sentence lede and two buttons, Open the Calculator (primary) and Start the guide (outline). The timeline heading starts below the fold.

FORM: Vertical age timeline with alternating tool tiles (round-2 mock F, chosen by the owner over the dealt structures), seed key 2d07a1a5. Signature interaction: the age line fills and its nodes light as you scroll; each tile's chart draws once about 90% of it is on screen and resets when it drops below the fold, so scrolling back up replays it.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
