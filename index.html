How this is calculated

  • Contributions land at the end of each period.
  • The annual rate of return is converted to an equivalent periodic rate, so money added during the year earns a partial year of growth.
  • Contribution growth is applied once per year, on the anniversary.
  • Inflation-adjusted figures state what the money would be worth in today's spending power.
  • The withdrawal shown is the first year of retirement.
  • A partial final period is dropped rather than counted whole.
  • The target solve uses a closed-form annuity, so its answer can sit a fraction of a percent off the period-by-period projection above.
